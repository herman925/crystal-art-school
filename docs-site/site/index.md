# Crystal's website guide

Use this guide when you want to change something on your website. Start with the task
below; each page tells you where to click, how to save and what to check afterwards.

Starting your review? Follow [Try your website before launch](/website-review). Record what
you could complete and anything that needs a change; an untested step is not a pass.

::: info Your editing account
Sign in to reach **Content Hub**. Your account has the content, course and agreed price/order/access tools described here. Site administration and technical settings stay with the site administrator.
:::

start with the WordPress editor; the service dashboards are for managing the relevant accounts.

## The routine for most changes

1. Sign in at [the website editor](https://crystalau.com/wp-admin/).
2. Open **Content Hub** and choose the relevant page or list.
3. Make the change, then use that editor's **Save**, **Update** or **Publish** button.
4. Open the saved website page and check it on a wide and a narrow screen.

Choosing a picture is not the same as saving the page. A lesson and its course also have
separate save buttons. [Saving and publishing](/editing-basics) explains the difference.

## If you get stuck

Keep your unsaved words, note the page and exact message, and [send them to the site administrator](/help).
If something you expect to edit is missing, ask the site administrator to check access before creating another copy.
