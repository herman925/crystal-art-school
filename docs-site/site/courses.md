# Courses

**Course content and course prices use separate workflows.** Use the course editor for titles, images, descriptions and lessons. Set or change paid-course prices only through the confirmed [Course prices workflow](/course-prices-draft), not the ordinary course-save button. Those money instructions remain a draft pending final review and handover.

| Field | Where it shows |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Course card and course page |
| **Featured image** | The cover on both |
| **Regular price / Sale price** | Both; change only through the confirmed [Course prices workflow](/course-prices-draft). A sale price crosses out the regular one |
| **Difficulty level** | The small chips under the title |
| **Category** | The tag above the title |
| **Description** | The paragraph under the course title |
| **What will I learn** | The "By the end of this course" list |
| **Targeted audience** | The "This course suits" cards |
| **Materials included** | The "What comes with it" list |
| **Topics and lessons** | The syllabus |

If a course has no materials listed and no files, the 'What comes with it' section is left out of the page.

## Two fields with a shape to follow

**Targeted audience** is split at a dash. Type `Complete beginners — you have never held a
brush` and the site shows the first half as a heading and the second as its explanation. No
dash means no explanation, which still works but reads flat.

**What will I learn** and **Materials included** are one item per line. Do not number them;
the site does that.

## Prices

Use **Course prices** and its separate review/confirmation step for paid-course amounts; follow the [draft instructions](/course-prices-draft) only after final review and handover. Enter numbers without a currency symbol, and leave Sale empty when no sale is intended.

The agreed course currency is GBP (£). **Currency and cart settings are managed by Herman**, not through your content-editing controls. Do not change them in Tutor Settings; ask Herman if anything appears wrong.

The lesson count and total runtime under each course are worked out from the lessons
themselves. You cannot type them, and they correct themselves when you add a lesson.

## Order of the courses

**Page attributes → Order** on each course, lowest first. Courses with no number fall back
to newest first.

The same order decides the row of courses on the homepage, which shows the first ten
published courses.

## The four extra photographs on a course

Besides the cover, a course can carry four smaller photographs. The homepage card picks one
of them at random each time the page is loaded, so the homepage keeps changing slightly
without you doing anything.

Two of your four courses — **Little House** and **Urban sketch** — have none of these
filled in. If one of them is published as it stands, its homepage card reads "Course
photographs to come" where the picture should be. Fill the four slots before publishing
either, or ask the webmaster to word it differently.

## The filters look after themselves

The Courses page has a column of filter and sort controls down the left. There is nothing to
set up: every button is built from the courses that are actually published.

A group of filters only appears when it can change what you see. All four courses are
watercolour, so there is no medium filter — one button that selects everything is worse than
no button. Tag a second course with a different medium and the group appears on its own.

The same goes for difficulty, length, price and the sale filter. Fill in a course properly
and it becomes findable; leave a field empty and the filter for it stays away.

## Who can watch a course

There are no membership levels. Someone who has paid for a course can watch that course's
video lessons and download its materials. Someone who has not can still read every public
page — the homepage, About, the gallery, the blog and the free videos.

## Draft and published

A draft is not a published course. For a new paid course, save the content as a draft, set and confirm its first price through **Course prices**, reload the same saved course, and publish only when the content, price and launch timing are agreed. A saved price does not publish the course.

For an already published course, use the same confirmed price process for money changes; do not use the ordinary content-save button to change its price. Follow the [draft Course prices guide](/course-prices-draft) after final review and handover, and ask Herman if the current course status is unclear.
