# The homepage banner

Open **Content hub → Home** and choose the homepage-banner link. It opens the approved slide settings. The account route still needs final checks and handover.

The banner is a carousel of **three slides**. Each slide has four fields you control:

- **Main title**
- **Description**
- **Large image**
- **Small image**

There are always exactly three slides. Adding a fourth or dropping to two is not something
the settings can do.

## About the two images

Each slide shows a large image and a smaller one overlapping it. The site crops whatever
you upload to fit its slot, so the layout does not break when you change a picture — but a
photo cropped to a shape it was not composed for can lose its subject.

The recommended size for each slot is written next to the field in Customize. Uploading
something close to that shape gives the best result.

## Before you publish

Use the preview beside the settings and check the banner at a narrow window width as well
as a wide one. The two images move relative to each other on smaller screens.
