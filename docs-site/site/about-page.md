# The About page

Two places. **Customize → Pages → About** holds your words and your portrait. **About
photos** in the dashboard menu holds the three photograph strips.

## Your words and your portrait

**Customize → Pages → About.**

| Field | Where it shows |
|---|---|
| **Opening paragraph** | The first paragraph, under your name |
| **Portrait photograph** | The picture of you at the top |
| **Quotation** | The pulled-out quote further down |
| **Artistic journey** | The first essay panel |
| **Community** | The second essay panel |
| **Meet your instructor** | The third essay panel |

Each box already contains what is on the site now, so you can read it and change what you
want rather than starting from a blank page.

The three essay boxes contain a little markup — `<p>` around each paragraph, `<em>` for
italics. Type between those marks and leave them where they are. If you would rather delete
the lot and type plain text with a blank line between paragraphs, that also works.

## One thing that will surprise you

**Emptying a box puts the old text back.** If you delete the quotation entirely and press
Publish, the original quotation reappears. The same happens if you press **Remove image** on
the portrait — the original portrait comes back.

This was built to stop a blank space appearing on a live page, but it does mean "delete
this" is not something these boxes can do. If you want a paragraph gone rather than
replaced, ask the webmaster.

## The portrait

It is cropped to a tall rectangle, so anything you upload fits. A photograph composed wide,
with you off to one side, loses the sides when it crops.

## The three photograph strips

**About photos** in the dashboard menu. One entry per photograph.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Describes the photograph for screen readers. Nobody sees it on the page |
| **Featured image** | The photograph. Required |
| **Which set** | On the right. Live drawing, Exhibition, or Artful Living |
| **Page attributes → Order** | Position in its strip, lowest first |

Each strip is filled independently. Until you publish the **first** photograph in a set, that
strip keeps showing the pictures that are on the site today. Publish one, and the whole strip
switches to yours — so add a set in one go rather than one at a time.

There are three strips and they cannot be added to or renamed. What goes in them is yours.

## Still fixed

**The awards and certificates.** Every row of that table, the scans and their captions are
not editable from the dashboard. Two reasons: they are public claims about your credentials,
and one of the scans is deliberately blurred to hide personal details. An open editing box
there is an easy way to publish something unchecked, or to replace the blurred scan with the
original by accident. Send changes to the webmaster.

**Section headings, the greeting and the buttons.** Part of the design.

**Two blocks of your writing** — the teaching philosophy section and the welcome cards near
the bottom — are still in code rather than in a box. That is an oversight rather than a
decision. Ask the webmaster and they can be opened the same way as the rest.
