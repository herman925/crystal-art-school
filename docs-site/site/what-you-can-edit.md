## Choosing, replacing and clearing pictures

Use **Choose image**, or **Replace image** when a picture is already selected. Choose an allowed public picture, then press **Use this image**. Choosing, replacing or clearing changes the form first; use its **Save …** button to save the change. These draft forms do not use the preview's **Publish** button.

Read the action beside each picture before clearing it:

| Picture | Clearing behaviour |
|---|---|
| Carousel large or small picture | **Clear slot** leaves that picture out of the slide; it does not restore a default |
| Optional TOOLS photograph | **Clear slot** leaves the plain dark card |
| Home About Crystal and free-video photographs, About portrait, and all three brand images | **Use shipped default** restores the original site picture, not your last replacement |

Neither action deletes a picture from the media library. The existing crop stays the same. If a picture is rejected, ask the webmaster rather than trying a private course file or another settings route.

## Leaving text or links empty

- **About text:** an empty field restores its original wording, rather than removing that paragraph.
- **Blog and Free Videos introductions, and Home text:** an empty field hides that text. Clearing a principle's title hides that principle and its text, but does not erase its saved text.
- **Carousel title and description:** cleared fields stay blank. Keep the first slide's title filled in; clearing it also leaves the homepage's main heading empty.
- **Contact email and social links:** cleared values stay empty; they do not restore defaults. An icon or link may remain with no destination, so clearing a value is not a way to hide it. Ask the webmaster if it should be removed from the layout.

If a save warning says the page changed or saving could not be confirmed, keep a copy of your unsaved text and follow the warning. Do not keep pressing Save; ask the webmaster if confirmation is unclear.

## Shared information

The contact settings feed several pages and the footer. Change the shared setting once rather than trying to maintain separate copies. The contact email is the public enquiry address used by those contact links; changing it is not the same as creating an inbox or setting up email delivery.

Published courses, artwork and articles supply parts of the homepage automatically. See [the rest of the homepage](/homepage-cards). A missing section may mean the necessary content has not been published, not that you need access to a design tool.
