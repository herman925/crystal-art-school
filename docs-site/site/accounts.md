# What students see

This page helps you recognise a student's journey. The login, basket, checkout and dashboard
layouts are maintained by Herman.

## Buying and learning

1. Read the course information and watch any lesson offered as a free preview.
2. Add the course to the basket and check its title and amount.
3. Sign in, or register when registration has been opened.
4. Complete checkout. The launch plan is card payments; PayPal is not part of these instructions.
5. Open **Your Studio → My courses**, then continue the lessons.
6. Find orders under **Purchases**.

A student may watch a preview without receiving paid materials. Notes and reference files
require access to the course. Removing a file's final course/lesson reference stops its old download link.

## If a student asks for help

Use [Sales](/sales) to find an order. Use [Course Access](/course-access) only for an agreed
access correction. A completed order, a recorded payment and course access are separate
states; a 100% coupon can produce a completed order with £0 due.

A saved order is not proof that a receipt email reached the student. If payment, access or
email delivery is unclear, collect the order number and [ask Herman](/help).

Registration, live payments and email opening checks stay with Herman. Keep the account
pages in place; they are not ordinary content pages to rename or delete.
