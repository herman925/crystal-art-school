# What students see

This page helps you recognise a student's journey. The login, basket, checkout and dashboard
layouts are maintained by the site administrator.

## Buying and learning

1. Read the course information and watch any lesson offered as a free preview.
2. Add the course to the basket and check its title and amount.
3. Sign in, or register when registration has been opened.
4. Complete checkout. The planned payment choices are card payments and PayPal. Use the choices actually offered at checkout; live payments open separately.
5. Open the studio dashboard and choose **My courses**, then continue the lessons.
6. Find orders under **Purchases**.

Registration may still be closed during the review. Use the student account prepared for the
session. Your editing account is for managing the website and is not a suitable check of what
an ordinary buyer can access.

## In the student's studio

- **My courses** lists courses that account can access. Open the intended course to continue learning.
- **Purchases** shows that account's orders and available receipt/invoice links. Check the order's
  course, amount and status; access and payment do not always have the same label.
- **Settings** contains the student's own details and Showcase preference. Account changes do not
  give access to somebody else's purchases.

If a video is waiting for browser permission, use [Privacy choices](/privacy-and-permissions#a-video-is-waiting-for-permission).
Once allowed, try play/pause, sound, seeking and full screen. On a phone, check that the player
and lesson details remain usable when the screen turns.

A student may watch a preview without receiving paid materials. Notes and reference files
require access to the course. Removing a file's final course/lesson reference stops its old download link.

## If a student asks for help

Use [Sales](/sales) to find an order. Use [Course Access](/course-access) only for an agreed
access correction. A completed order, a recorded payment and course access are separate
states; a 100% coupon can produce a completed order with £0 due.

A saved order is not proof that a receipt email reached the student. If payment, access or
email delivery is unclear, collect the order number and [ask the site administrator](/help).

Registration, live payments and email opening checks stay with the site administrator. Keep the account
pages in place; they are not ordinary content pages to rename or delete.

For the coordinated review, follow [the student checks](/website-review#_6-recognise-the-student-experience).
