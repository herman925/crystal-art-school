# Privacy and student permissions

There are two separate choices to understand: permission to publish a student's work or
credit, and the visitor's choice to load external video players in their browser.

## Publishing a student's work

Get permission for the photograph and its intended public credit before publishing.
An account on the website is not permission to publish that person's work or details.

In **Student Work**, link the correct **Registered Student**. If the picture may be published
but their name should be omitted, choose **Hide public name credit** while keeping that link.
If permission to publish the picture is unclear, keep the entry as a draft.

Students can save their own Showcase ON/OFF preference in the studio's **Settings → Student
Showcase**. OFF excludes their linked work from future wall visits, even when the name is
hidden. Previously downloaded pictures and direct public image addresses may remain;
contact the site administrator if the image itself must be withdrawn everywhere possible.

The default in **Site-Wide Edit → Privacy & Showcase settings** applies only to students
without a saved preference. It does not override their choice and is not consent on their behalf.
[Student work instructions](/showcase).

## A video is waiting for permission

External video players start off until the visitor enables them for that browser.
They can use the permission control beside a waiting player, **Privacy choices** in the
footer, or **Manage browser privacy** in studio settings. Enable **External video players**
and return to the film.

The browser remembers the choice for six months when storage is available. Another browser,
device or private window can have a different choice. Enabling the player does not buy a
course or grant access to its paid files. Turning it off unloads the external player on that
page; it cannot remove information already held by the video service. This choice controls
external players, not every third-party connection on the website.

The privacy panel also shows Essential, Analytics and Marketing. Essential services remain
required. Analytics and Marketing are inactive when not configured; the panel is not a
promise that those services are installed or that legal wording has been approved.

## Requests about personal information

The data-request buttons prepare an email for the visitor to send. Opening the email does
not submit the request automatically. If you receive one, send its details to the site
administrator through the agreed private channel so it can be handled and recorded.

Do not remove a student's account or change their course access as an improvised response
to a privacy request. [Student support tools](/money-guide) and account administration have
different purposes.

## Before sharing a screenshot

Show the message and relevant control. Leave out passwords, card details, sign-in links,
private video addresses and student details that are not needed to understand the problem.
