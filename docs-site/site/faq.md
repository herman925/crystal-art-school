# Questions and answers

**FAQ** in the dashboard menu. Each question is its own entry.

- The **title** is the question.
- The **body** is the answer. Bold, links and lists all work.
- **Page attributes → Order** sets the position. Lower numbers come first.

Manage the entries available to your account. The FAQ page shows published entries. If an expected question is missing, ask the webmaster to check access rather than creating a duplicate.

## Answers that are not yours to guess

Six subjects need a definite, checked answer rather than a reasonable-sounding one:

- which payment methods work
- the refund window
- how long access lasts
- whether anything can be downloaded, and how many times
- what device or software a student needs
- what happens when a payment fails

Whatever is typed into an FAQ entry becomes the site's promise about these, and a student
can hold you to it. Confirm the wording with the webmaster before publishing changes to those six.

Two answers currently on the site are waiting on exactly this — one about how long access
lasts, one about paying by card without a PayPal account. Both are flagged with the webmaster.
