# Questions and answers

**FAQ** in the dashboard menu. Each question is its own entry.

- The **title** is the question.
- The **body** is the answer. Bold, links and lists all work.
- **Order** sets the position. Lower numbers come first.

Manage the entries available to your account. The FAQ page shows published entries. If an expected question is missing, ask the site administrator to check access rather than creating a duplicate.

## Add or change an answer

1. Open **Content Hub → FAQ → Manage Questions** and choose the question, or **Add FAQ Entry** for an agreed new one.
2. Enter the question as the title and its answer in the main writing area.
3. Set **Order** if its place in the list should change. Lower numbers come first.
4. Save a draft while unfinished; publish a new answer when ready, or save/update the existing one.
5. Open the public FAQ page and expand that question to check its answer and links.

Updating the editor's title/date sort does not change the public Order. Moving a question to
Draft or Trash withdraws it from the public list.

## Answers that are not yours to guess

Six subjects need a definite, checked answer rather than a reasonable-sounding one:

- which payment methods work
- the refund window
- how long access lasts
- whether anything can be downloaded, and how many times
- what device or software a student needs
- what happens when a payment fails

Whatever is typed into an FAQ entry becomes the site's promise about these, and a student
can hold you to it. Confirm the wording with the site administrator before publishing changes to those six.

Use the currently agreed wording. If a policy needs changing, ask the site administrator to check it
before publishing a new promise.
