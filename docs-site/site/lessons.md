# Lessons

Start from **Content hub → Courses** and open the relevant course's lesson controls. Work only on courses available to your content account. The exact routes and controls still need final checks and handover.

Each lesson has three things you control:

- **Title** — shows in the syllabus
- **Content** — the one-line description under the title in the syllabus. Keep it short;
  this is a subtitle, not an article
- **Video** — the film for that lesson

## About the videos

Course films are hosted separately from the site's photographs and text. Each lesson's approved video setting points to the intended film. Access checks and sharing restrictions must be maintained by the webmaster; do not assume that possessing a video address is permission to share it.

If you need to replace a film, that is a job for the webmaster — the new file has to be uploaded to
the video service first and the lesson pointed at it afterwards.

Students cannot leave comments, questions or notes under a lesson at the moment. That is switched off on purpose.

## Free preview lessons

A lesson explicitly marked as a preview may be watched without buying the course. Check which lessons are intended to be public rather than relying on an old count. Preview video does not make paid course downloads freely available. Agree a preview change before publishing it.
