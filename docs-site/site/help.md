# If something is not working

## First, keep your work

Copy any unsaved words somewhere safe. Note which page or entry you were editing.
Do not delete another entry or change an unrelated setting to make the screen look right.

## Send the site administrator these details

1. The page, course title or order number.
2. What you were trying to change.
3. The exact message, or a screenshot showing it.
4. Whether you pressed the final Save/Confirm button, and approximately when.

Keep student details out of the screenshot unless they are needed to identify the problem.
Never include passwords, card details or a confirmation link.

## Common situations

| What happened | What to do |
|---|---|
| An expected course or entry is missing | Ask the site administrator to check ownership/access; do not make a duplicate |
| The page says someone else changed it | Keep your unsaved words, reload and compare before editing again |
| A saved picture does not look right | Check its crop and the saved website page; the original upload is still in Media |
| An image is already used by public content | Upload a separate copy for the paid course file |
| No clear result after a money/access confirmation | Stop, check a fresh list without changing it, and send the site administrator the message |
| A student reports a payment problem | Record their order number and exact message; do not tell them to pay again |
| A student has not received an email | Ask the site administrator to check delivery; repeating an order/access action is not a way to send mail |

For price, coupon or access warnings, see [money and access help](/money-help).
