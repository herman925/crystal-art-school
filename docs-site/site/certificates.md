# Your certificates and honours

Open **Content Hub → About → Certificates**. Each entry is one row in the honours table
on About, with its own scan and caption.

The display follows three rules:

- **One pin, or none.** A pinned scan gets the large highlight and its row comes first.
  Pinning another replaces the previous choice.
- **Then Order, lowest first.** The remaining entries follow their saved Order numbers.
  The Year does not decide their position.
- **No scan still means a visible honour.** A published, Public entry can show its year
  and wording without a scan. Visitors can read it, but cannot open a scan or see it as
  the large highlight.

1. Open an existing entry, or choose **Add Certificate**.
2. Put the honour or exhibition wording in the **title**.
3. In **Certificate Details**, enter the **Year** and **Scan Caption**. The caption goes
   beneath the scan; the title is the wording in the table. A blank caption uses the title.
4. Use **Featured image** to upload or choose the public scan. To replace it, remove the
   old selection and choose the replacement.
5. Set **Order** under **Post Attributes**. Lower numbers come first.
6. **Publish** a new entry when ready, or **Update** an existing published entry.
7. Open About and check the row, caption and full-size scan.

An entry without a scan can still show its table wording; see [If no scan is provided](#if-no-scan-is-provided).
A draft stays off the public page.
Use **Public** visibility for entries you want on About. Other visibility choices hide
the entry from About; they do not make an uploaded scan private.

Upload only the version you want visitors to see. Cover signatures or personal details
before uploading when needed. The existing masked scan is already preserved; selecting
a replacement makes that new image the public version.

## Change the order

Open an entry, change **Order**, then **Update**. For example, 10, 20 and 30 leave room
to put a later entry at 15. The Year does not sort the list automatically.

The public About page sorts entries in this sequence:

1. The pinned certificate, if it has a public scan.
2. All other entries by **Order**, lowest number first: 0, then 10, then 20, for example.
3. If Order numbers match, the entry added to the dashboard first comes first.

For example, pinning an entry with Order **30** puts it before entries with Order **0**,
**10** and **20**. Unpinning it returns it after those entries. Its saved Order stays **30**.

Changing a Year or caption does not reorder the entries. Sorting the dashboard list by
Title or Date changes that dashboard view only; it does not rearrange About.

Save the certificate and its scan first. Return to the **Certificates** list.
On a phone, use the arrow beside the entry to reveal its **Highlight** column.

1. Find the intended published certificate.
2. In its **Highlight** column, choose **Pin to top**.
3. Check About: it now appears in the large highlighted position and first in the table.

**Only one certificate can be pinned at a time, and you can have none.** Pinning a
different certificate replaces the previous choice; it does not delete the previous
entry or change either entry's Order number.

Choose **Unpin** to remove the large highlight. Every entry then follows its saved Order.
Pin to top changes the public About page; the dashboard list can have a different sort.

A published, Public entry still shows its **Year** and **title wording** in the honours
table. Missing a scan does not hide that wording or turn the entry into a draft.

- On a wide screen, its scan space shows **Scan not supplied**.
- Visitors cannot open a full-size image from that row.
- **Pin to top** is unavailable until a public scan is saved.

You can supply the scan later: open the same entry, choose its **Featured image**, then
**Update**. There is no need to create another entry. Keep it in **Draft** if you do not
want even its wording to appear yet.

If you remove a pinned entry's scan and Update, its wording stays in the table but the
large highlight disappears. The dashboard may say **Pinned choice; not currently shown**.
Adding a scan again restores that selected highlight; use **Unpin** to clear the choice.

## Withdraw an entry

Move it to **Draft** or **Trash** to remove it from About. Withdrawing the pinned entry
also removes its public highlight. This does not delete the uploaded scan from Media.

The existing rows marked **Demo** are layout samples. They stay published for review
until their launch treatment is agreed. An exhibition poster remains labelled as a poster.
