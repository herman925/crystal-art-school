# Your certificates and honours

Open **Content Hub → About → Certificates**. Each entry is one row in the honours table
on About, with its own scan and caption.

1. Open an existing entry, or choose **Add Certificate**.
2. Put the honour or exhibition wording in the **title**.
3. In **Certificate Details**, enter the **Year** and **Scan Caption**. The caption goes
   beneath the scan; the title is the wording in the table. A blank caption uses the title.
4. Use **Featured image** to upload or choose the public scan. To replace it, remove the
   old selection and choose the replacement.
5. Set **Order** under **Post Attributes**. Lower numbers come first.
6. **Publish** a new entry when ready, or **Update** an existing published entry.
7. Open About and check the row, caption and full-size scan.

An entry without a scan can still show its table wording. Its scan cannot be opened or
pinned until an image is saved. A draft stays off the public page.

Upload only the version you want visitors to see. Cover signatures or personal details
before uploading when needed. The existing masked scan is already preserved; selecting
a replacement makes that new image the public version.

## Change the order

Open an entry, change **Order**, then **Update**. For example, 10, 20 and 30 leave room
to put a later entry at 15. The Year does not sort the list automatically.

The pinned certificate always leads the public table. The other entries follow Order,
lowest first. Entries with the same Order keep their existing entry order.

Save the certificate and its scan first. Return to the **Certificates** list.
On a phone, use the arrow beside the entry to reveal its **Highlight** column.

1. Find the intended published certificate.
2. In its **Highlight** column, choose **Pin to top**.
3. Check About: it now appears in the large highlighted position and first in the table.

Only one is pinned. Pinning a different certificate replaces the previous choice.
Choose **Unpin** to remove the large highlight and let Order decide the full list again.

## Withdraw an entry

Move it to **Draft** or **Trash** to remove it from About. Withdrawing the pinned entry
also removes its public highlight. This does not delete the uploaded scan from Media.

The existing rows marked **Demo** are layout samples. They stay published for review
until their launch treatment is agreed. An exhibition poster remains labelled as a poster.
