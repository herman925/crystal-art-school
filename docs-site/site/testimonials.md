# Student comments on the homepage

**Testimonials** in the dashboard menu. By default, up to three appear on the homepage. **Content Hub → Home → Edit Home → Student Comment Limit** lets you set a display limit from **1 to 12**. Use **Save Home** to apply it; extra comments remain saved.

Each entry has two parts:

- **The body** — what the student said. Type it in as plain text.
- **"Who said it"** — a dropdown on the right listing the available member accounts. Pick the student.

You do not type the student's name or upload their picture. Both are read from that
student's own account, so if they change their display name or photo it updates here
automatically, and it can never disagree with what they see elsewhere on the site.

**Order** controls which comments show, up to your display limit, lowest numbers first.

If nothing is published, the section disappears from the homepage rather than showing an
empty space.

## Add, change or withdraw a comment

1. Open **Content Hub → Home → Student Comments**, then the intended entry or **Add New**.
2. Enter the agreed comment in the main writing area and select its student in **Who said it**.
3. Set **Order** to choose its position among the published comments.
4. Save a draft until approved, then publish the new entry or save/update an existing one.
5. Check the homepage's quote, credit, picture and position. The Home display limit may hide later entries.

Move the entry to Draft or Trash to withdraw that comment. This does not remove the student
account or change their course access. If a required student is missing from the chooser,
ask the site administrator rather than creating another account.

## One thing to check first

These cards publish a real student's name and profile picture on your public homepage. Make
sure the student has agreed. The site administrator is separately sorting out the wording in the member
terms that covers this.
