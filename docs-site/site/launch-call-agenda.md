# Launch call: agenda and what to prepare

This is a 45-minute online call to review outstanding launch questions and agree the next steps. Bring the information you have; the call is not permission to open payments or registration before their checks are complete.

## The agenda

1. **Welcome** — 2 minutes. Introductions and what we will cover.
2. **Business Registration and Stripe checks** — 7 minutes. Where your Business Registration
   stands, and the identity check that Stripe asks for before it can pay you.
3. **Your Stripe payout account** — 3 minutes. Checking together in your Stripe dashboard whether a pounds bank account can be added for payouts.
4. **Tax adviser answers** — 6 minutes. UK VAT, plus three questions about the wording we use for no refunds, for your tax adviser.
5. **Course titles and prices** — 5 minutes. Going through the names and prices, one course at a time.
6. **Purchase terms, privacy policy and member terms** — 5 minutes. Checking the wording on each page.
7. **Course notes and reference photos** — 5 minutes. Which notes and photos go with each course.
8. **The gallery (optional)** — 3 minutes. Only if you have time. Painting details.
9. **Your old Wix site and the domain plan** — 3 minutes. What happens to the old address when the new site goes live.
10. **Launch date** — 4 minutes. Agreeing a realistic date together.
11. **Wrap-up** — 2 minutes. What we agreed and who does what next.

Total: 45 minutes.

## Please have these ready before the call

- Your Business Registration details, or a note of where it stands.
- Your Stripe account open on your own screen, signed in. You will not need to share your log-in details with us.
- Your tax adviser's contact details, or any written answers they have already given you.
- Your ideas for course titles and prices, even rough ones.
- The course notes and photos you already have, in any folder you can find.
- Your current website address.

## Already decided, so we will not spend call time on them

- Card payments only at launch (PayPal can come later).
- All prices in pounds.
- Receipt delivery must be checked before payments open.
- Email confirmation for new students must be checked before registration opens.

## After the call

- We update the "Before launch" checklist page, so you can see each item and its status.
- We send you a short summary of what was agreed and what is still open.
