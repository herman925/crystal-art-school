# Logo, signature and contact details

> **Draft — the new editing forms are awaiting final checks on the working website and account handover. They have not been verified live.** Use them only after the webmaster confirms they are ready for your account.

Start from **Content hub → Every page** and choose the approved logo/signature or contact link. These open only the settings available to your content account, not the full Appearance menu.

The upcoming **Site-wide brand images** and **Site-wide contact and social links** forms use their own **Save …** buttons. The older **Customizer** preview uses **Publish** instead; that is a different screen, not the save step for the new forms. Follow the route confirmed for your account rather than trying another settings screen.

## Logo and signature

Three separate images, used in three places:

| Setting | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| **Header logo** | Top left of every page |
| **Hero signature mark** | Inside the banner at the top of most pages |
| **Footer signature** | Bottom of every page |

They are separate on purpose — the header logo is small and wide, the signature is a
different shape. Swapping one does not swap the others.

The hero signature mark is shared by the pages that use it. Replacing it affects those
pages, not just the page you are looking at.

In the draft brand form, use **Choose image** or **Replace image**, then **Use this image** and **Save Site-wide brand images**. **Use shipped default** restores that field's original site image, not your last replacement or a blank space. It does not delete the image from the media library. The existing layout and file-upload restrictions stay the same.

## Contact and social links

Four settings: **Instagram URL**, **Facebook URL**, **YouTube URL**, **Contact email**.

Paste the full web address for the social links, starting with https://. The site works
out what to display from the address, so you do not type your handle separately.

These four values feed the footer of every page, the FAQ page, the About page and the
homepage strip. One change updates their existing uses across the site. **Contact email** is the public enquiry address used by those contact links; changing it does not create an inbox or set up email delivery.

Cleared social links and contact email stay empty; they do not restore defaults. An icon or contact link can remain with no web address or email destination. Do not clear a value just to hide it — ask the webmaster if it should be removed from the layout. In the draft contact form, press **Save Site-wide contact and social links** after editing.
