# Try your website before launch

Use this page for your website review with the site administrator. The aim is to check that
you can complete your everyday tasks and that students can find and use the finished content.
Keep this guide open in one tab and the website editor in another.

## Before you start

- Use your own website editing account. Use the separate student account supplied for learner checks.
- Agree which draft entries, course and student account are for practice. The published demo entries
  remain available for review; they are not automatically assigned to your editing account.
- Changes to a published page or entry can appear on the website immediately. Use the agreed draft
  for practice and record any public change that needs restoring.
- For price, coupon, order and access tests, use the records and steps prepared for the review.
  Complete checkout only when the site administrator has confirmed the test payment method.

Reviewing the website does not itself open registration, live payments or search-engine indexing.
If a step is unavailable, record it as **Not tested** and say why.

## 1. Find your way around

Sign in and find **Content Hub**. Open an editing guide from this guide's task list. Check that
you can tell which page to use and which button saves it. Try guide search with a phrase such
as **certificate**, **lesson video** or **course price**. Select a screenshot to enlarge it.

Expected: your assigned content and support tools are available, and you do not need another
person's login. [Signing in](/logging-in) · [Find the right editor](/what-you-can-edit).

## 2. Change words and a picture

On the agreed page or draft, change a short piece of writing. Choose or replace a picture and,
where offered, try **Reposition**. Save with that editor's button. Reopen it, then inspect the
saved website page on a wide screen and a phone.

Expected: the intended words and picture remain saved; the crop keeps its subject; links open
the intended destination. [Saving and publishing](/editing-basics) · [Pictures and files](/pictures-and-files).

## 3. Review About and certificates

Find the biography and the three photograph sets. In the agreed certificate entry, check the
title, year, caption, scan and Order. If pin changes are part of the review, record the current
pin first and restore the agreed selection afterwards.

Expected: only one certificate is highlighted, or none. Remaining rows follow Order. A public
honour without a scan can still show its wording; it has no image to open.
[About](/about-page) · [Certificates](/certificates).

## 4. Prepare a course and lesson

Open the agreed course. Find **Basics**, **Curriculum** and **Additional**. Identify the
**Course Card Cover**, five gallery photographs and separate **Course Highlights Video**.
Open a lesson with its pencil and check its own title, description, cover and exercise files.

If an upload is included, use the agreed short sample film. Wait for **Ready**, preview it,
save the lesson and reopen it. Save any separate course changes too. If recovery is included,
follow the same-file resume instructions with the site administrator.

Expected: the lesson save retains the intended film and duration. A public highlight is chosen
only after its public-preview confirmation. Uploading a film does not publish a draft.
[Courses](/courses) · [Lessons and files](/lessons).

## 5. Review the other content lists

Try the agreed draft article, free-video entry, artwork, FAQ and student comment. For student
work, check the photograph, correct registered student and permission to publish. Inspect the
saved public pages where publishing is part of the review.

Expected: draft content stays out of public lists. Published content appears according to its
picture, category, order and privacy rules. Home's course and article rows fill themselves.
[Blog](/blog) · [Free videos](/free-videos) · [Gallery](/gallery) · [Student work](/showcase)
· [Student comments](/testimonials) · [FAQ](/faq).

## 6. Recognise the student experience

Use the supplied student account in a separate browser or private window, rather than your
editing account. Check the course catalogue, an available preview, the studio's **My courses**
and **Purchases**, a lesson and its materials. Try play/pause, seek, sound and full screen.
If a player is waiting for permission, check that browser's privacy choice.

The site administrator coordinates registration, checkout, receipts, refunds and the checks
for a student who has not bought the course. A previous test payment is not permission to
try a real payment during this review.

Expected: the right student can access the right course and files. A preview does not unlock
paid materials. [What students see](/accounts) · [Privacy and student permissions](/privacy-and-permissions).

## 7. Find the support tools

Locate **Course Prices**, **Coupons**, **Sales** and **Course Access**. Use the agreed test
records to practise any approved change, reading the review before the final confirmation.
If the session only covers finding an order or reading a screen, stop there and record that scope.

Expected: you can distinguish a price, payment, order status and course access, and know which
requests go to the site administrator. [Prices, orders and access](/money-guide).

## Record each result

Use **Pass**, **Needs a change** or **Not tested**. Keep one report per problem:

| Detail | What to write |
|---|---|
| Task | The step you were trying to finish |
| Page or entry | Page address and course, lesson or entry title |
| Expected | What you thought should happen |
| Actual | What happened, including the exact message |
| Device | Phone/computer and browser |
| Evidence | A screenshot with only the necessary details |
| Save or confirmation | Whether you pressed it, and roughly when |

Do not include passwords, card details, sign-in links or full private video addresses.
If a save, payment or access result is uncertain, stop and follow [Help](/help).

## Finish the review

Agree which practice drafts should stay, which public changes should be restored, and who
will do that cleanup. Keep the published demos until their treatment is agreed. Review the
remaining content, business and launch decisions in [What to prepare before launch](/before-launch).
Sign out of the website and this guide when finished.
