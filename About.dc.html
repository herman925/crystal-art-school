✦ About

Hi, I'm Crystal.

I've taught small-group painting classes in Hong Kong for eight years, and this school is the online version of that same room — one lesson at a time, real reference material, and all the time in the world to get the big shapes right before the small ones.

Between lessons I post full painting demonstrations on YouTube and share a study nearly every day on Instagram — come see how slowly I really paint.

Come paint with me.

✦ Artist biography

Crystal Lau

Crystal Lau is a Hong Kong–based painter and teacher working primarily in oils and watercolour. Her work lingers on quiet, everyday subjects — harbour light, studio corners, the worn tools on a working table — painted with a patient, value-first approach.

She studied fine art before spending eight years running small-group classes from a compact studio of plaster, wood and iron. Her paintings have shown in group exhibitions across the city, and her studies now reach a wider audience online.

  • Based in Hong Kong
  • Media Oil · Watercolour · Drawing
  • Teaching 8 years, small-group

Terms & privacy

Purchases & refunds+

Courses are one-time purchases with lifetime access — no subscription, no expiry. Prices are shown in GBP and include VAT where applicable.

Full refund within 14 days of purchase if you've watched less than a quarter of the course. Email hello@crystalsartschool.com from your account address and the refund is processed to your original payment method within 5 working days.

Using the course materials+

Course videos, notes and downloads are licensed for your personal study. You're welcome — encouraged! — to paint from the reference photos and share your paintings anywhere, credited or not.

The videos and written materials themselves may not be re-uploaded, re-sold or shared outside your household. Teachers wanting to use materials in a classroom: write to me, I'm usually happy to arrange it.

Your account & your data+

Your email is used for receipts, course access and — only if you opt in — the occasional studio letter. No marketing lists, no data sold to anyone, ever.

Under UK GDPR you can request, correct, export or delete your data at any time from your account settings, or by emailing me. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.

Cookies+

Essential cookies keep you signed in and remember your cart. Optional analytics cookies help me see which lessons people get stuck on — you choose in the cookie banner, and can change your mind anytime via "Privacy choices & data rights" in the footer of the cookie panel.
