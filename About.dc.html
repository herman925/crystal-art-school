✦ About

Hi, I'm Crystal.

I've taught small-group painting classes in Hong Kong for eight years. Online, I want to keep that same slow, careful rhythm: one lesson at a time, with real reference material, and room for questions.

✦ Artist biography

Crystal Lau

Crystal Lau is a Hong Kong–based painter and teacher working primarily in oils and watercolour. Her work lingers on quiet, everyday subjects — harbour light, studio corners, the worn tools on a working table — painted with a patient, value-first approach.

She studied fine art before spending eight years running small-group classes from a compact studio of plaster, wood and iron. Her paintings have shown in group exhibitions across the city, and her studies now reach a wider audience online.

✦ In her words

My story

I didn't come to teaching in a straight line. For years I painted quietly after work, filling sketchbooks on the train and chasing the light through my studio window. Painting was the thing that slowed the week down.

When a few friends asked me to show them how, those evenings turned into small classes — and the classes turned into a studio. I started posting demonstrations on YouTube and sharing daily studies on Instagram, and slowly a whole community of unhurried painters gathered around them.

This school is the online version of that same room: real reference, real demos, and all the time in the world to get the big shapes right before the small ones.

✦ How I teach

Slow, in the right order

01

Big before small

Shapes before features, values before colour, gesture before anatomy. Every course is sequenced so the early decisions protect the later ones.

02

Real reference, real demos

Every lesson ships with the exact photos I paint from and the palette I mix — so you can follow along stroke for stroke, or go your own way.

03

Room for questions

Fellows get a private Discord channel where I look at block-ins, answer colour questions, and run monthly critiques.
