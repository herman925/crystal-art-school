Hi, I'm Crystal.

I've taught small-group painting classes in Hong Kong for eight years. Online, I want to keep that same slow, careful rhythm: one lesson at a time, with real reference material, and room for questions.

✦ Artist biography

Crystal Lau

Crystal Lau is a Hong Kong–based painter and teacher working primarily in oils and watercolour. Her work lingers on quiet, everyday subjects — harbour light, studio corners, the worn tools on a working table — painted with a patient, value-first approach.

She studied fine art before spending eight years running small-group classes from a compact studio of plaster, wood and iron. Her paintings have shown in group exhibitions across the city, and her studies now reach a wider audience online.

✦ In her words

My story

I didn't come to teaching in a straight line. For years I painted quietly after work, filling sketchbooks on the train and chasing the light through my studio window. Painting was the thing that slowed the week down.

When a few friends asked me to show them how, those evenings turned into small classes — and the classes turned into a studio. I started posting demonstrations on YouTube and sharing daily studies on Instagram, and slowly a whole community of unhurried painters gathered around them.

This school is the online version of that same room: real reference, real demos, and all the time in the world to get the big shapes right before the small ones.

✦ Questions, answered

FAQ

How do the courses work?+

Each course is a series of pre-recorded video units — watch them anytime, as often as you like. Every unit ships with the exact reference photos I paint from and downloadable notes, so you can follow along stroke for stroke or take your own route.

I'm a complete beginner — where do I start?+

Start with Materials & setting up — it's completely free, all six units. It covers what to buy (very little), how to set up, and your first wash. After that, any beginner-tagged course will feel comfortable.

What's the difference between Apprentice and Fellow?+

Both are per-course purchases. Apprentice gets you the full course — videos, references, notes, lifetime access. Fellow adds access to my private Discord channel, where I look at your block-ins, answer colour questions and run monthly critiques.

Do I need expensive materials?+

No. My list is deliberately short — fewer than ten things, and most students already own half of them. One honest tip: cheap paint on good paper beats good paint on cheap paper, every single time.

Can I get a refund if a course isn't for me?+

Yes — within 14 days of purchase, as long as you've watched less than a quarter of the course. Just email me and I'll sort it, no forms.

Can I buy your original paintings?+

You can — everything in the gallery shop is available as an original or limited print. The Buy button on each piece takes you straight to the Etsy listing, where checkout and shipping are handled.

Terms & privacy

Purchases & refunds+

Courses are one-time purchases with lifetime access — no subscription, no expiry. Prices are shown in GBP and include VAT where applicable.

Full refund within 14 days of purchase if you've watched less than a quarter of the course. Email hello@crystalsartschool.com from your account address and the refund is processed to your original payment method within 5 working days.

Using the course materials+

Course videos, notes and downloads are licensed for your personal study. You're welcome — encouraged! — to paint from the reference photos and share your paintings anywhere, credited or not.

The videos and written materials themselves may not be re-uploaded, re-sold or shared outside your household. Teachers wanting to use materials in a classroom: write to me, I'm usually happy to arrange it.

Your account & your data+

Your email is used for receipts, course access and — only if you opt in — the occasional studio letter. No marketing lists, no data sold to anyone, ever.

Under UK GDPR you can request, correct, export or delete your data at any time from your account settings, or by emailing me. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.

Cookies+

Essential cookies keep you signed in and remember your cart. Optional analytics cookies help me see which lessons people get stuck on — you choose in the cookie banner, and can change your mind anytime via "Privacy choices & data rights" in the footer of the cookie panel.
