Free videos

Over eight hours of free painting lessons — real, complete tutorials, not previews. Pick a subject below, press play, and paint along at your own pace. When you're ready for the full slow-paced series, the step-by-step courses pick up where these leave off.

Let's feel the fear… and paint it anyway.

Still life tutorials

Still life is where the fundamentals live — balancing colours, building a painting in layers, working from a coloured ground. One object on a table can teach you most of what a landscape will ask of you later.

▶Part 1 of 4

A single apple, four washes

The first still life I give every beginner — one object, four layers, and all the fundamentals of watercolour glazing.

 ▶2 parts

Light & shadow on simple forms

Making any object feel solid before you touch a single detail — the principles of form and realism, demonstrated slowly.

 ▶21 min

A copper pot in poster colour

Capturing the brilliant reflections of metal with three values and a steady hand.

Landscape tutorials

Ever wondered how to paint rain, or balance vivid greens in a scene? These lessons work with a muted palette to capture the changing moods of the seasons.

▶17 min

A muted field, wet-on-wet

Controlling soft edges in a simple field study — where to let the water do the work, and where to stop it.

 ▶Part 1 of 3

Rain over the harbour

Value relationships in a grey-day scene — proof that a "dull" subject makes the strongest painting.

 ▶3 studies

Skies in loose watercolour

Three sky studies in thirty minutes — clouds are the best teacher of confident, unfussy washes.

Colour mixing tutorials

Many things in nature are far more muted than the tube suggests. These lessons look at light against dark and warm against cool — the differences that matter more than bright colour.

▶14 min

Mixing greens from scratch

Why tube green fails you outdoors, and the two-pigment fix that never does.

 ▶12 min

Warm vs cool greys

The quiet power of a limited value palette, mixed live on the desk.

 ▶9 min

Mixing a chromatic black

Don't buy black — mix a richer, livelier one from two tubes you already own.
