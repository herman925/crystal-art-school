✦ Questions, answered

Ask me anything.

The things people usually want to know before picking up a brush — courses, materials, refunds and the Discord. Anything missing, just write to me.

How do the courses work?+

Each course is a series of pre-recorded video units — watch them anytime, as often as you like. Every unit ships with the exact reference photos I paint from and downloadable notes, so you can follow along stroke for stroke or take your own route.

I'm a complete beginner — where do I start?+

Start with Materials & setting up — it's completely free, all six units. It covers what to buy (very little), how to set up, and your first wash. After that, any beginner-tagged course will feel comfortable.

What's the difference between Apprentice and Fellow?+

Both are per-course purchases. Apprentice gets you the full course — videos, references, notes, lifetime access. Fellow adds access to my private Discord channel, where I look at your block-ins, answer colour questions and run monthly critiques.

Do I need expensive materials?+

No. My list is deliberately short — fewer than ten things, and most students already own half of them. One honest tip: cheap paint on good paper beats good paint on cheap paper, every single time.

Can I get a refund if a course isn't for me?+

Yes — within 14 days of purchase, as long as you've watched less than a quarter of the course. Just email me and I'll sort it, no forms.

Can I buy your original paintings?+

You can — everything in the gallery shop is available as an original or limited print. The Buy button on each piece takes you straight to the Etsy listing, where checkout and shipping are handled.
