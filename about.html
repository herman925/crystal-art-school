✳ About

Hi, I'm Crystal.

I've taught small-group painting classes in Hong Kong for eight years. Online, I want to keep that same slow, careful rhythm: one lesson at a time, with real reference material, and room for questions.

I paint in oils and watercolour, draw every day, and believe almost everyone can learn this — given patience and the right order of things.

Come paint with me.

✳ How I teach

Slow, in the right order

01

Big before small

Shapes before features, values before colour, gesture before anatomy. Every course is sequenced so the early decisions protect the later ones.

02

Real reference, real demos

Every lesson ships with the exact photos I paint from and the palette I mix — so you can follow along stroke for stroke, or go your own way.

03

Room for questions

Fellows get a private Discord channel where I look at block-ins, answer colour questions, and run monthly critiques.
