1. Project Overview
Crystal Art School is a project to build a professional website that serves two purposes:
A personal profile for the art teacher with a warm, artistic feel
An online course platform where students can browse, purchase, and watch art lessons
This proposal is for building a new website at crystalau.com — a modern, responsive platform built on WordPress. The new site will support course sales, student accounts, video lessons, and a blog.
Target audience: Hong Kong and UK students interested in art education. Year 1 target: 0 to 50 enrolled students.

4. Key Decisions Explained
Why WordPress?
WordPress is the most widely-used website platform in the world. It gives full control over the site, is easy to find developers for in the future, and has thousands of plugins for added features. It is not tied to any specific hosting company.
Why Tutor LMS for the course platform?
Tutor LMS is a purpose-built plugin for online courses on WordPress. It handles student accounts, course progress tracking, quizzes, and works directly with WooCommerce for payments. The free version covers all core needs for launch.
Why Bunny.net for video hosting?
Bunny.net charges only for what is used. At the expected scale (0-50 students in Year 1), costs are estimated at approximately HK$25-55 per year. This is much cheaper than fixed monthly plans, and the service includes video protection features.
Why Elementor Pro for building pages?
Elementor Pro is the industry-standard page builder for WordPress. It allows fast creation of custom page layouts without needing to write code. The 'One' plan (approximately HK$1,300 per year) includes all features needed for this project.
Why manual Discord invites for Fellows?
Students who purchase a 'Fellow' tier course will see a Discord invite link on their dashboard after purchase. The teacher approves the invite manually in Discord. This is simple, reliable, and does not require custom development. An automated bot can be added later if desired.
Why a single launch instead of a soft launch?
The site will be built on a password-protected staging environment until fully ready. Only when everything is tested and approved will the site go live publicly. This avoids exposing unfinished work to potential customers.

12. Common Questions
Answers to questions that clients often ask:
Question
Answer
Can I sell course bundles?
Yes, the paid LMS version will support bundles. It will be added later with a paid upgrade.
Can students pay with PayPal?
Yes. Both Stripe (card payments) and PayPal are included.
Can I change course prices after launch?
Yes. Prices can be updated at any time through the WordPress dashboard.
What happens if a student asks for a refund?
Refunds are handled manually by the site administrator. The platform will allow for submission of refund request.
Can I add more courses after launch?
Yes. The platform supports unlimited courses. Adding courses after launch has no extra development cost.
Do I need to buy the Tutor LMS or Elementor plans right away?
Tutor LMS free version and Elementor free version can be used initially. Paid plans unlock more features and are in scope.
What if I want to change website developers later?
WordPress is the world's most common website platform. Any WordPress developer can take over from this one.
How do students access my Discord channel?
After purchasing a Fellow-tier course, students see a Discord invite link on their dashboard. The teacher approves each invite manually.
Can I use my own domain name?
Yes. crystalau.com will be registered (given if it’s not used) and set up for the new site. crystalauart.com will remain on its current platform — this is a brand-new build, not a migration.
Is the website mobile-friendly?
Yes. All pages are built to work on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
What if I need help after the site launches?
One month of technical support is included after launch. After that, support can be quoted separately.
